# Initial use-case research

Storyboard Studio will start with the **private decision brief**: a consultant,
product lead, or operations lead needs a concise, credible deck to align a
small group without sending an unfinished brief to a hosted workspace.

## Observation protocol

Before changing the wedge, run the same 15-minute task with 8–12 people:

1. Give the participant a synthetic decision (for example, choose a first
   30-day onboarding improvement).
2. Ask them to create a 3–5-slide brief with the no-key local planner.
3. Observe where they revise the narrative, whether the exported text is
   usable, and what evidence they expect to carry into the room.
4. Ask what they would share with a colleague and what would stop them from
   running the tool locally.

Do not collect presentation content, telemetry, or identifying information by
default. Record only consented, anonymized observations in the table below.

## Initial proxy walkthroughs

These eight synthetic walkthroughs are a starting hypothesis, not claims of
user interviews. They cover the intended audience until consented sessions are
available.

| Persona | Job to be done | Success signal | Friction to validate |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Independent consultant | Frame a recommendation for a client | Client can repeat the decision and next step | Can the consultant trust local-only handling? |
| Product lead | Align a team on one roadmap choice | Meeting ends with an owner and milestone | Are source/evidence fields sufficient? |
| Operations lead | Explain a process change | Staff understand the before/after | Do comparison blocks help? |
| Enablement lead | Prepare a short internal workshop | Participants see one coherent sequence | Is the 5-slide default the right length? |
| Privacy-sensitive researcher | Draft a briefing from private notes | No network request is needed | Is the local fallback useful without AI? |
| Startup founder | Pitch a focused next experiment | Audience sees the problem and ask | Does the deck feel specific, not generic? |
| Engineering manager | Secure agreement on an investment | Trade-offs are explicit | Are decision blocks legible in viewers? |
| Customer-success lead | Propose an onboarding change | Customer outcome is concrete | Can a non-developer install in minutes? |

The first template and README language are intentionally specific to this
hypothesis. Replace proxy findings with consented observations before broadening
the product.
