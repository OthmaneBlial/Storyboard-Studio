# Case study: a private decision brief (synthetic)

## Situation

A product or operations lead has one meeting to align a small group on a
change. Their raw notes contain context and uncertainty, but they need a calm
three-slide narrative rather than a pile of generated slides.

## Workflow

1. Enter the decision and desired outcome in the local planner.
2. Review and edit the title, trade-off, bullets, layout, and block before any
   file is created.
3. Add an author-owned evidence label, export a native PPTX, and refine it in
   PowerPoint or LibreOffice.

## Observed outcome

The synthetic fixture makes the decision, comparison, and next-step sequence
visible in under five minutes. It demonstrates the product contract; it is not
an external customer claim. Replace this observation with a consented,
anonymized case study before using it as a quantified marketing result.
