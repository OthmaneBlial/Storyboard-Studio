# Curated templates

Templates are small, source-grounded starting points — not “AI research”. Each
one includes a brief, a validated outline, and an explicit statement of the
evidence assumptions a person must replace.

## Private decision brief

- Input: [`examples/templates/decision-brief.json`](../examples/templates/decision-brief.json)
- Job: align a small group on one decision and next step.
- Narrative: name the decision → make the trade-off clear → turn agreement into action.
- Evidence assumption: all claims and options are placeholders supplied by the
  author; Storyboard Studio does not verify them.

Generate it locally with:

```bash
storyboard export --input examples/templates/decision-brief.json --output output/decision-brief.pptx
```
