# Maintainer playbook

Storyboard Studio is maintained on a best-effort basis:

- triage new issues within 14 days when possible;
- publish a small release or a status update at least once per quarter;
- label proposals as renderer, accessibility, documentation, template,
  security, provider, or question;
- close declined or stale proposals with a reason and a pointer to the product
  contract, not a silent deletion;
- thank contributors in release notes and record the verification they ran.

The maintainer may enable Discussions when there is capacity to answer them.
Until then, the issue forms and `SUPPORT.md` are the canonical support path.
Pull requests retain the local-first, privacy, editability, and compatibility
checklist in `.github/PULL_REQUEST_TEMPLATE.md`.
